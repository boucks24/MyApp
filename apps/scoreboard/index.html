Scores come from ESPN's public scoreboard feeds, fetched directly from your browser (this page has no server of its own). Those feeds are unofficial, undocumented, and can change or go down without notice — if a section stops loading, that's ESPN's endpoint, not something saved on this device. Tennis is filtered client-side to ATP/WTA 500, Masters/1000, and Grand Slam events by tournament name; that keyword list lives in TENNIS_500PLUS_KEYWORDS near the top of the script and may need a touch-up each season as the tour calendar shifts. Cricket, rugby union and other Australian representative fixtures aren't wired up yet — their ESPN endpoint codes weren't verifiable when this was built; add a new entry to the SOURCES array once you've confirmed one.
