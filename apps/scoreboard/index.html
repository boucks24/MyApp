Scores come from ESPN's public feeds, fetched directly from your browser (this page has no server of its own). Those feeds are unofficial, undocumented, and can change or go down without notice — if a section stops loading, that's ESPN's endpoint, not something saved on this device. ATP and WTA are fetched separately (a tour's own single-gender events only appear under its own endpoint) but merged into one Tennis list and deduped, since a Grand Slam's payload already carries both genders together. ESPN's tennis "events" are whole tournaments spanning weeks, not single matches — extractMatchesFromTournamentEvent() pulls each singles-draw match out of a tournament's groupings[] (doubles/mixed/wheelchair/juniors are skipped, since they have no ATP/WTA singles ranking to filter by) and drops anything outside the live/upcoming window so finished early rounds don't flood the list. What's left is filtered to Grand Slam, Masters/1000 and tour-500 events — an explicit major: true flag covers Slams, a name-keyword list (TENNIS_500PLUS_KEYWORDS) covers the rest — then to matches with at least one player ranked top 20 (curatedRank.current, embedded right on each competitor in the scoreboard payload — no separate rankings API needed) or Australian (by athlete.flag, any rank). Both of those tennis filters fail open: if the tier or ranking/nationality filter would have matched nothing despite matches actually being on, it shows everything unfiltered at that stage instead and says so inline — a filter matching zero real matches is treated as the filter being broken, not the schedule being empty. Non-tennis sources are requested 2 months ahead; tennis stays on its own shorter window since a tournament payload already spans its own multi-week event. Rugby union covers Super Rugby Pacific (Australian Super Rugby teams) and the Rugby Championship (Wallabies internationals) by numeric ESPN league ID. Cricket uses a different, cross-domain "personalised header" endpoint filtered to matches involving Australia — Big Bash/WBBL aren't wired up, their league ID wasn't verifiable when this was built. Review each season and adjust SOURCES/TENNIS_500PLUS_KEYWORDS as calendars and endpoints shift.
