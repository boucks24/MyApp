Scores come from ESPN's public feeds, fetched straight from your browser, so there is no server and nothing is stored anywhere but this device. Those feeds are unofficial and undocumented: if a section stops loading, that is the endpoint, not your data. Tennis is filtered to Grand Slam, Masters and 500-level events featuring a top-20 or Australian player, and both filters fail open, showing everything unfiltered with a note rather than a misleadingly empty list.
