Everything runs in your browser and nothing is uploaded. The pipeline seeks to each sampled frame individually rather than streaming, so detections are never dropped to keep up with playback. Longer or higher-rate clips take longer; capped at 900 sampled frames. The pose model and 3D library are fetched from a CDN on first run, so the reconstruction step needs a network connection even though your video never leaves the device.
