2. Detection settings

Uses TensorFlow.js to spot the ball and a player's swinging wrist frame-by-frame, as a second opinion alongside motion/sound. Downloads ~15–20MB of models on first use and is considerably slower — a general-purpose model, not one trained on tennis, so treat it as a rough extra signal rather than a reliable ball tracker.

On others (including iOS Safari) there's no way to keep that link, so you'll be asked to re-pick the same file when you want to play it back.
