2. Detection settings

Uses TensorFlow.js to spot the ball and a player's swinging wrist frame-by-frame, as a second opinion alongside motion/sound. Downloads ~15–20MB of models on first use and is considerably slower — a general-purpose model, not one trained on tennis, so treat it as a rough extra signal rather than a reliable ball tracker.

Daily wellness —

Stress score through the day
This is a background health export, not a recorded activity — no GPS/route. Resting heart rate is usually just one or two readings a day (shown as a stat above, not a chart); stress is sampled roughly every minute, so it's the one series worth charting. Garmin's other daily-summary fields (steps, floors, detailed calories) aren't reliably documented outside their own SDK, so this stays to the field decoded with real confidence rather than guess at scale factors for the rest.
